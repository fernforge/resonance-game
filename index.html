HOW TO PLAY

  • Place nodes on the grid. Pick a node from the tray (bottom), then click a cell. Each costs energy.
  • A global Pulse ticks. On the beats it's tuned to, a node fires at nearby enemies — and plays a note. Your whole board becomes music.
  • Dissonance flows toward your Core. Don't let it through.
  • Behind your notes runs a chord progression (named in the top bar — every run gets its own, like ♪ AURORA). A bassline follows it and the board pulses its colour each time the chord turns over, so your defense isn't just rhythmic — it's a whole evolving song you can share.
  • Silence is power. A node that rests charges up — its next hit crits (white flash). Firing the same node every beat fatigues it (each repeat hits softer). Spaced, syncopated parts out-damage a flat wall of sound.
  • RESONANCE (top bar) measures how musical your board is — sparsity, syncopation, and variety between parts. It sets how high Groove can climb, so a monotone "everything on every beat" board is the weakest build, not the strongest. Compose interlocking rhythms; hit the GROOVE ⟳ button on a node for instant patterns.
  • Tuning dozens of nodes? Hit ✦ MAESTRO (bottom bar) to auto-arrange the whole board into one interlocking groove in a click — tap again to re-roll. Or tune one node and press ⬢ ALL TYPE in its popup to stamp that groove across its whole section.
  • Kill enemies on the beat to build Groove — a score multiplier and pure style.
  • Node types synergize: Amplifiers buff neighbors (no beats — a warm pad), Relays have no beats of their own and echo any neighbor that fires, Resonators hit huge but only on a beat where a neighbor also fires (sync).
  • Click a placed node to retune which beats it fires on (relays just echo, so there's nothing to set), or spend energy to UPGRADE it — leveled nodes hit harder, so you can grow taller instead of just wider.
  • Every node is a real note (shown in its popup, e.g. E5). Tune it with PITCH ▼/▲ (step up/down the scale), OCT ▼/▲ (jump an octave), and ♭ / ♮ / ♯ (bend a semitone for chromatic colour). A node's pitch also rises with its row (higher = higher) and its level — so leveling up for power also lifts the note; drop it back with PITCH ▼ to forge a powerful low voice. Notes are tinted by pitch on the board, so you can see the melody. The board is genuinely an instrument — it can play a real tune in key (see FUR-ELISE.md).
  • An enemy's COLOUR is its note — match it to shatter. Every enemy is tuned to a pitch from the live chord and wears that pitch as its colour (the same hue your nodes glow). A node's pulse carries its note. When the colours line up, the note resonates — the enemy SHATTERS for huge bonus damage (a bright ring in its own colour, a bell, "♪ +score"). When they clash (a tritone or semitone apart), the note is DISSONANT and barely scratches (a dull grey shield clink). So a one-note wall can only kill one colour of enemy — a varied, rainbow board that plays a real chord covers the whole swarm. Pitch isn't decoration: colour = note = damage.
  • ✦ SHIFTERS keep a varied board honest. The five-sided Shifter (from wave 6) re-tunes itself every couple of seconds — its colour flips to a new chord tone while it's still walking in, so the single note that shattered it a moment ago now barely scratches. You can't park one perfect note against it; only a board that already covers several colours always has the right answer ready. Variety isn't optional — it's the counter.
  • Buffs sing, too. An adjacent Amplifier lifts a node a clean octave (stronger and brighter), and when your RESONANCE climbs past 75% the whole board rises an octave — playing well literally makes the machine soar. Every buff effect is octave-based, so it never knocks your tune out of key. ✦ MAESTRO also balances pitch into a symmetric arch across the board, so a tidy, mirrored layout sounds as composed as it looks.
  • Every 5 waves the board grows a ring bigger, so your machine never runs out of room. Survive a wave, then draft a new node or upgrade. Go as long as you can.
  • Every 10th wave is a BOSS wave — the Conductor arrives last and shatters into a swarm when it falls. Big risk, big payout.
  • Every run earns Resonance Shards. Spend them in UPGRADES for permanent head starts that carry across runs.
  • Try the Daily Challenge — a fixed seed + twist that's the same for everyone that day. Chase your best and share the result card.
  • Share your song. Hit ⧉ COPY BOARD (bottom bar) and a one-click link to your exact board lands on your clipboard. Anyone who opens it drops straight into your composition — every pitch, octave and rhythm rebuilt note-for-note. (No link? You'll get a short R1~ code instead — a friend pastes it under ⧉ LOAD CODE on the menu.) The board is the song, so the song is what you share.
